[SCHOLARSHIP AND FEE WAIVER CIRCULAR – 2026]

1. SCHOLARSHIPS AVAILABLE

1.1 Merit Scholarship
Students scoring above 90% aggregate in the previous semester examinations are eligible for the Merit Scholarship. 
The scholarship covers 50% of the tuition fee for the following semester.
Applications must be submitted by October 5th through the student portal.

1.2 Need-Based Scholarship
Students from families with an annual income below Rs. 3,00,000 are eligible for a need-based scholarship.
Required documents: income certificate (issued by a Gazetted Officer), latest bank statement, and the scholarship application form.
Submit all documents to the Financial Aid Office before September 15th.

2. FEE PAYMENT PORTAL

All fees must be paid via the official student portal at portal.institution.edu.
Accepted payment modes: Net Banking, UPI, Debit Card, and Credit Card.
Fee receipts are automatically emailed to the registered student email within 24 hours of payment.

3. REFUND POLICY

A full refund of tuition fees is available if a student withdraws before the end of the first week of classes.
A 50% refund is applicable if withdrawal happens within two weeks.
No refund is issued after the second week of the semester.

4. EXAMINATION FEE

The examination registration fee is Rs. 1,200 per semester.
Students must register for examinations through the student portal at least 30 days before the examination dates.
Late registration is subject to an additional fee of Rs. 300.
