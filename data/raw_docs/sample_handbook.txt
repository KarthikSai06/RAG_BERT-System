[INSTITUTIONAL HANDBOOK 2026]

1. ADMISSIONS
1.1 Eligibility
To be eligible for undergraduate programs, a student must have passed their 12th standard (or equivalent) examination with a minimum of 60% aggregate marks.
For postgraduate programs, a bachelor's degree with at least 55% aggregate is required.

2. FEES AND PAYMENTS
2.1 Semester Fee Deadline
The deadline to pay the even semester fees is March 15th of every year. The odd semester fee deadline is August 20th.
A late fee of $50 is applied if paid within 15 days after the deadline. After 15 days, the student may not be allowed to attend classes.

2.2 Fee Waivers
Fee waivers are available for students from economically weaker sections. To apply for a fee waiver, a student must submit the income certificate of their parents along with the fee waiver application form to the administrative office by September 1st.

3. EXAMINATIONS
3.1 Attendance Requirement
A minimum attendance of 75% in each subject is mandatory to be eligible to sit for the end-semester examinations.
Medical leaves up to 10% can be considered, subject to the submission of a valid medical certificate from a registered practitioner within one week of rejoining.

4. HOSTEL
4.1 Application Process
Students must apply for a hostel room online through the student portal before July 10th. Room allocation is based on distance from hometown and academic merit.
Hostel fees must be paid in full before moving in.

4.2 Rules
Consumption of alcohol or illegal substances in the hostel premises is strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate expulsion.
Curfew timing for all students in the hostel is 10:00 PM. No student is allowed to enter or leave the hostel after 10:00 PM without prior written permission from the warden.
